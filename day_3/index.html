STEP 3 · TRANSCRIPT NOTES

Listen again with these cues

Situation: The presenter talks about struggling to understand fast speech.

Problem: In real conversations, asking for repetition may not solve the problem because the speaker can repeat the same sentence at the same speed.

Strategy: Instead of pretending to understand, briefly summarise what you think you heard and ask smaller questions. This helps the other person explain the missing information.

Useful phrases: “So the first thing I have to do is …?” · “Which office is that again?” · “Can you repeat that, please?”
