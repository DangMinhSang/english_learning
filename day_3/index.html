DAY 3 · LISTENING

Train your ear for real English.

Listen to a B1 conversation, test your understanding, then use the script to review the parts you missed.

🎧 1 listening⏱ 30–45 min🎯 B1 level
STEP 1

Listen

Listen once without the script. Then listen again and focus on the main idea and key details.

An interview about listening skills

B1 · British Council LearnEnglish

STEP 2

Check your understanding

Answer before reading the script. After checking, read the feedback under each question.

1. Why does the presenter find listening stressful?

2. What problem can happen when asking someone to repeat?

3. Which situation is described as more important or high-stakes?

4. What strategy does Gabriella recommend?

5. What is the main lesson?

STEP 3

Script / transcript

Read this study version after your first attempt.

Presenter: Listening can feel stressful, especially when people speak quickly or use unfamiliar expressions.

Gabriella: Asking someone to repeat can help, but sometimes they simply repeat the same sentence at the same speed.

Gabriella: In an important situation, such as dealing with paperwork at a bank or a government office, it is better to show what you understood and ask a smaller, focused question.

Useful strategy: Briefly summarise what you think you heard, then ask for the exact piece of information you are missing.

Useful phrases: “So the first thing I have to do is …?” · “Which office is that again?” · “Can you repeat that, please?”
