A Small Change in My Daily Routine

Last year, Minh decided to change his daily routine. He often stayed up late and felt tired in the morning. He also spent too much time checking social media before going to bed.

He started with one simple rule: no phone for thirty minutes before bedtime. Instead, he prepared his clothes for the next day, read a few pages of a book, and wrote down three things he needed to do the following morning.

After a few weeks, Minh noticed that he fell asleep more easily. He also had more energy when he woke up. The change was small, but it helped him feel more organized and less stressed during the day.

Minh learned that improving a habit does not always require a big plan. Sometimes, one small action repeated every day can make a real difference.
