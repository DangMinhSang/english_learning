ENGLISH JOURNEY · DAY 5

Speaking: Talk about your daily routine

Build confidence with a simple B1 speaking task. Plan your ideas, speak for 1–2 minutes, and self-check your performance.

🗣️ Speaking⏱ 30–45 minB1 beginner
STEP 1

Prepare your ideas

Speak about one useful habit in your daily life. Use the prompts to make short notes before you start.

Describe one habit that helps you study, work, or stay healthy.
Include these pointsWhat the habit is · when you do it · why it helps · one example from your life
Useful starters“One habit I have is …” · “I usually do it when …” · “It helps me because …” · “For example, …”
STEP 2

Speak for 1–2 minutes

01:30
You can record yourself with your phone or speak aloud now.
STEP 3

Self-check

Tick every statement that is true after you finish speaking.

STEP 4

B1 model answer

One habit that helps me study is making a short plan before I start. I usually do this in the evening or before an important task. First, I write down two or three things that I need to finish. Then I choose the most important one and work on it for about thirty minutes.

This habit helps me because I feel less stressed and I do not waste time deciding what to do. For example, when I have an English lesson, I prepare my notebook, review the previous words, and choose one small goal. It is a simple habit, but it helps me study more regularly.

Lesson plan

1. Prepare · 10 min2. Speak · 5 min3. Self-check · 10 min4. Repeat · 10 min

Self-scoring

3 checks = good start
1–2 checks = repeat with shorter sentences
