Writing: Describe a useful habit

Write a short B1 paragraph about a habit that helps you study, work, or feel better.

✍️ Writing⏱ 30–45 minB1 beginner
STEP 1

Understand the task

Write 80–100 words about one useful habit. Explain what the habit is, when you do it, why it helps, and one small result you have noticed.

Prompt

Describe a habit that helps you study or work better.

Include: what the habit is · when you do it · why it is useful · one result.

STEP 2

Plan before writing

1. OpeningSay what the habit is: “One useful habit I have is …”
2. DetailsUse time phrases: every morning, before class, after dinner.
3. ReasonUse because, so, and this helps me to …
4. ResultFinish with a simple result: “As a result, I …”
STEP 3

Useful language

One useful habit I have is …Một thói quen hữu ích của tôi là …
I usually … before/after …Tôi thường … trước/sau khi …
This helps me to …Điều này giúp tôi …
As a result, …Kết quả là …
It is simple, but it makes a difference.Nó đơn giản nhưng tạo ra sự khác biệt.
STEP 4

Write your paragraph

Write your answer below. Try to use at least three linking words: because, so, also, but, as a result.

0 wordsTarget: 80–100 words
STEP 5

Sample B1 answer

One useful habit I have is preparing my study plan every evening. I usually spend ten minutes writing down the things I need to do the next day. This helps me to start work quickly because I already know my priorities. I also prepare my books and laptop before I go to bed. As a result, I waste less time in the morning and feel more relaxed. It is a simple habit, but it makes a big difference to my study routine.
